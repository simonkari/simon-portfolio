What I'm Building

Mama's Kitchen

Ordering page for a home catering side hustle

Mama's Kitchen took orders through WhatsApp, calls, and Instagram DMs, with no visible menu or prices and messages getting lost in the scroll while she cooked. I built a single mobile-first page where customers can see today's menu, place an order in under a minute, and send it straight to her WhatsApp as a ready-to-send message. There's no app and no payment system, just a clean front door.

42 Online Orders in 6 Weeks · Order-related messages down ~60%

  • Next.js
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Framer Motion
  • WhatsApp API
